## Overall judgment

The hypothesis is materially better than the old V2 surface-label collapse. It correctly identifies brand-world construction, process/action, semantic information tiers, campaign sequencing, and broader context.

However, it still overweights layout/information architecture and underweights several mechanisms that materially contribute to the reference's visual identity:

- low-key cinematic process photography;
- warm heat/light contrast against deep dark fields;
- controlled hot accent color as structural emphasis;
- display lettering acting as visual mass, not merely text content;
- integration of typography, photography, graphics and tonal hierarchy as one system.

It must be revised before becoming a Provisional Visual Program.
