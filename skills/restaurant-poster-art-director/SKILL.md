# Restaurant Poster Art Director

版本：`2.0.0`
状态：`MANDATORY`

## 1. Scope

This Skill applies to:

- one restaurant poster;
- one product hero visual;
- one food advertising key visual;
- several independent poster alternatives;
- a text-free product or visual asset for a brand project;
- a Xiaohongshu poster case or design breakdown.

It does not control a complete ten-page brand case. Full brand cases read `DAILY_AUTOBRAND_SYSTEM_V4_3.md` and the V4 structural files.

Editable Figma is not a default requirement. Enter the Figma route only when the user explicitly requests an editable Figma source, components, variables, or node IDs.

## 3. Mission

Create a commercially credible poster from a brand-specific concept, a truthful product, controlled composition, and human-finished typography.

The poster must not merely look polished. It must explain why this visual belongs to this product, this shop, and this commercial scene.

## 4. Required concept brief

Before image generation, record:

```text
product_fact:
consumer_scene:
business_problem:
brand_point_of_view:
core_concept:
visual_mechanism:
why_this_mechanism_belongs_to_this_brand:
```

Do not start from generic labels such as `高级`, `年轻`, `自然`, `新中式`, `松弛感`, or `电影感` without defining the mechanism.

## 5. Reference visual DNA

For important references, extract:

- composition;
- focal order;
- color and temperature;
- lighting;
- lens and crop behavior;
- materials;
- typography behavior;
- graphic mechanism;
- negative space;
- information density;
- brand-specific assets that cannot be copied.

Do not copy a reference's brand name, logo, supergraphic, watermark, studio footer, contact information, QR code, or complete commercial appearance.

## 6. Three-direction rule

Even when the user requests one final poster, compare at least three internal directions before selection.

Each direction must differ in at least four of these dimensions:

- concept;
- visual mechanism;
- photography;
- composition;
- typography behavior;
- material language;
- color personality;
- emotional tempo;
- extension behavior.

Color-only, font-only, mockup-only, and crop-only variants are not new directions.

When the user explicitly asks for several independent alternatives, the alternatives shown to the user must also be genuinely different.

## 7. Product-driven concept

The visual structure must come from a specific product shape, material, vessel, motion, cooking action, price logic, or consumption scene.

The product must affect:

- the layout skeleton;
- image crop;
- focal movement;
- graphic rhythm;
- typography direction;
- negative space;
- supporting marks.

Do not place a product into a generic template and then add decorative smoke, ink, seals, splashes, or English text.

## 8. AI generation boundary

AI may generate:

- text-free product key visuals;
- text-free environments and backgrounds;
- illustrations;
- textures;
- lighting and composition studies;
- isolated assets for later layout.

AI must not be trusted for final:

- Chinese characters;
- logo or wordmark;
- English and pinyin;
- prices;
- menu information;
- addresses, telephone, awards, dates, sales claims, or legal text;
- QR codes;
- final page hierarchy;
- production dimensions.

Final type, logo, prices, hierarchy, dimensions, and production layout require controlled manual typesetting with real fonts or vector paths.

A complete AI-generated poster remains `ASSET_DRAFT` until the final layout is rebuilt and verified.

## 9. Real product policy

For a real shop or real dish, prioritize real photography for:

- dish structure;
- portion;
- ingredients;
- vessel;
- garnish;
- cooking state;
- actual packaging.

AI may extend the scene, background, atmosphere, illustration, or campaign world. It must not invent a dish the shop cannot serve or silently change product facts.

## 10. Product hero audit

Reject:

- malformed anatomy or product shape;
- copied, fused, or cloned ingredients;
- plastic, waxy, rubber, or metallic food texture;
- glue-like sauce;
- impossible steam or smoke;
- floating products;
- impossible bowl, pan, packaging, or table perspective;
- inconsistent light and reflections;
- missing or false contact shadows;
- fake text embedded in a reusable visual asset.

Product realism outranks decoration.

## 11. Composition

Use one primary focal point and a clear reading order:

1. brand or key message;
2. product hero;
3. core claim;
4. minimal support.

Do not default to `headline + English subtitle + three round icons`.

The copy must not overpower the visual. The image must not depend on long explanation to become meaningful.

## 12. Typography

- AI text is placeholder only;
- final Chinese, English, pinyin, price, product name, and key claim require real typesetting;
- reject wrong characters, malformed strokes, fake seals, gibberish, and drifting logos;
- verify text at 100% zoom;
- verify final pixels and safe margins;
- verify contrast and small-screen readability.

## 13. Extension check

Before final selection, test whether the poster direction can also support:

- storefront or signage;
- menu or ordering interface;
- packaging;
- mobile cover or Xiaohongshu first image.

A single attractive poster that cannot extend to a coherent system is marked `REJECTED_ISOLATED_PRETTY_IMAGE`.

## 14. Independent-poster batch rule

Only when the user explicitly requests several independent poster alternatives:

- define at least four style families for ten alternatives;
- no family exceeds 40%;
- restrained Eastern styles do not exceed 30% unless requested;
- dark-black designs do not exceed 20%;
- every alternative still needs a product-specific concept and truthful product structure.

This quota must never be applied inside one coherent brand case.

## 15. Xiaohongshu publishing objective

Before writing the title or caption, declare one primary objective:

- `COLLECTION`;
- `DISCUSSION`;
- `CONVERSION`;
- `NOT_FOR_PUBLICATION`.

### Collection

Provide a clear object, a useful takeaway, an accurate search phrase, and a first image worth saving.

### Discussion

Provide a specific judgment, two pieces of visible evidence, and one narrow choice question.

### Conversion

Provide a real operating problem, a design tradeoff, and a verified result or consequence.

A conflict headline is not mandatory. Generic hooks without strong visual evidence are rejected.

## 16. Hard reject list

Reject:

- fake Chinese or malformed strokes;
- fake or drifting logos;
- plastic food, fake steam, or glue-like sauce;
- incorrect lighting, reflection, perspective, or contact;
- decorative seals, ink, smoke, splashes, or grain without concept logic;
- mechanical symmetry or cloned elements;
- dirty gray, fake ageing, excessive sharpening, or generic HDR;
- generic `Chinese premium` styling with no proprietary memory;
- three directions that are only color variants;
- a full AI page presented as final design;
- an attractive image that cannot extend;
- a generic hook unsupported by evidence;
- a broad question such as `大家觉得怎么样`.

## 17. Delivery

Default ready-to-post Xiaohongshu output when no other size is specified:

- `2160 × 3840 px`;
- PNG master;
- optional JPEG publishing copy;
- 100% zoom inspection.

User-specified dimensions take priority.

Use statuses:

- `CONCEPT_REVIEW`;
- `ASSET_DRAFT`;
- `LAYOUT_DRAFT`;
- `READY_FOR_QA`;
- `READY_TO_POST`;
- `REJECTED`.
