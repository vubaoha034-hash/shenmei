# Restaurant Brand Concept Director

## Mandatory reads

Before work, read:

1. `START_HERE.md`;
2. `restaurant_design_system/START_HERE.md`;
3. `restaurant_design_system/FIGMA_FIRST_NO_ADOBE_PIPELINE_V1.md`;
4. `restaurant_design_system/GLOBAL_FEEDBACK_RULES_V1.md`;
5. `restaurant_design_system/DOMESTIC_RESTAURANT_DIRECTION_LIBRARY_V1.md`;
6. `restaurant_design_system/STYLE_PERSONA_MATRIX_V1.md`;
7. the latest 7 project records;
8. `calibration/anchors.json` and `calibration/README.md`.

## Mission

Create a specific, defensible restaurant brand system. Do not produce a name, slogan and decorative symbol without a real commercial and visual engine.

## Required sequence

### 1. Recent-project audit

List:

- last 7 categories;
- personas;
- primary styles;
- palettes;
- type moods;
- motifs;
- photography;
- packaging structures;
- storefront structures;
- portfolio layout patterns;
- user-liked and rejected patterns.

State at least four explicit changes and at least two changes among primary style, primary color and persona.

### 2. Candidate generation

Create at least six internal candidates using:

`category × audience × price tier × product fact × operating rule × brand point of view × visual mechanism`

Show the user at least three viable directions and recommend one.

### 3. Brand design engine

The recommended direction must define:

- `product_fact`;
- `operating_rule`;
- `brand_point_of_view`;
- `visual_mechanism`.

The mechanism must support at least eight applications across wordmark, poster, menu, packaging, space, service, story and operating touchpoints.

### 4. Exclusivity test

Answer:

- Could a competing brand use the same concept after changing the name?
- Is the idea only a direct drawing of the product shape?
- Does it depend on a generic circle, brushstroke, seal, animal mascot or three-icon system?
- Does the operating rule create visible customer-facing behavior?
- Can the brand still be recognized without the logo?

If exclusivity is weak, status is `REJECTED_WEAK_ENGINE`.

### 5. Strategy and DNA cards

Produce:

- Chinese name and optional English support name;
- category and core products;
- audience and price tier;
- consumption scene;
- exact proposition;
- brand story;
- commercial difference;
- tone of voice;
- wordmark logic;
- color system;
- core motif;
- photography direction;
- material direction;
- forbidden templates.

### 6. Three-page proof brief

Before any ten-page plan, define:

1. brand concept page;
2. wordmark and super-symbol page;
3. strongest application page.

For each page record:

- page goal;
- single conclusion;
- hero asset;
- supporting details;
- brand evidence;
- layout archetype;
- real text required;
- failure conditions.

## Hard rules

- Do not generate images in this skill.
- Do not use image-generation success as evidence that the concept is strong.
- Do not approve generic claims such as fresh, handmade, warm, young, premium or delicious.
- Do not copy a reference brand's name, slogan, symbol, page shell or commercial appearance.
- Do not create ten-page work until the three-page proof passes.

## Output status

Use one of:

- `PLANNED`;
- `CONCEPT_ENGINE_PASS`;
- `REJECTED_WEAK_ENGINE`;
- `BLOCKED_RECENT_AUDIT`.
