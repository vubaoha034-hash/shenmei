## Result

The refinement is materially better than the pre-refinement Family Transfer artifact, but it does not yet qualify as a final production Golden Exemplar.

### Improvements that are visibly real

1. **Left identity rail is more controlled**
   - The previous full-height title block was visually heavy and generic.
   - R1 creates a clearer hierarchy between `今日`, the large `现烧`, and the supporting `锅气入味` line.
   - The identity rail now behaves more like a designed brand column than a generic vertical headline.

2. **Dish mass is better balanced**
   - The plated dish is smaller and less dominant.
   - The process scene, dining context and final dish now share attention more plausibly.
   - This improves breathing room and reduces the previous hero-dish pressure.

3. **Narrative modules are less scrapbook-like**
   - The three large circular windows/icons were removed.
   - A single restrained curved-line system with sparse semantic cues now connects process, ingredients and dining context.
   - The composition reads more as one authored system and less as a collage board.

4. **Dark field is cleaner**
   - Blacks are more coherent and less muddy than the previous artifact.
   - Warm firelight is retained without turning the whole image into a red/brown wash.

5. **Family identity remains intact**
   - Process cooking, multi-tier information, semantic ingredient/heat cues, and dining-world context remain visible.
   - The artifact does not regress to `large brush title + plated dish`.

## Remaining blockers

### 1. Display typography is now the dominant weakness

The main `现烧` lettering is more distinctive than before, but it still does not feel like a fully owned brand display system. It has a hybrid editorial/display character that is usable as direction, but not yet strong enough to become a canonical reusable identity asset.

This is no longer mainly a layout problem.

### 2. Functional typography and micro-copy still feel generated

The English labels (`WOK HEAT`, `CHILI AROMA`, `DINING ALIVE`) and supporting microtext are visually cleaner, but still read as generic support copy rather than a rigorously typeset brand information system.

### 3. Material and food realism are good but not reference-level photography

The wok/process scene is strong and believable, and the final dish is credible. However the overall image still has a slightly hyper-real rendered sheen and controlled cinematic uniformity compared with a top-tier real commercial photography campaign.

### 4. Final editorial precision is not yet top-tier

The artifact has a coherent authored system, but optical spacing, baseline rhythm, micro-alignment, and the relationship between display type and functional text still need deterministic production precision.

## System implication

The main remaining bottleneck has moved out of macro layout / family-program distillation.

The next high-information test is not another whole-image redesign. It is a **production-layer separation test**:

- strongest visual model continues to own photography, scene, macro composition and artistic display intent;
- deterministic production tooling owns exact functional typography, micro-copy, alignment and repeatable identity placement;
- display lettering may be generated as a dedicated visual asset, but should not be hand-drawn by Figma/Codex.

This follows the evidence from prior R1E failures: Figma/Codex low-level vector drawing is not a high-end Chinese display-lettering generator.
