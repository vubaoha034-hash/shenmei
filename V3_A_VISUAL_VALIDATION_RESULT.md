The artifact is a real structural improvement over the earlier V2 outputs. It clearly escapes the previous `large brush title + single food plate` collapse. The composition now has a real process hero, a middle campaign/module band, supporting information rhythm, and a bottom dining-world context.

- The middle red field is used as an information/campaign connector, not merely as a decorative brush patch.

   - It lacks the reference's more organic relationship between photographs, illustrated symbols, labels, and semantic motifs.

4. `BOTTOM_SCENE_DENSITY`

   - Its right-side icon rail is coherent but feels appended rather than fully integrated into the scene.

5. `AESTHETIC_FINISH_GAP`
   - The result reads as a competent brand campaign board, not yet as a top-tier authored visual identity system.
