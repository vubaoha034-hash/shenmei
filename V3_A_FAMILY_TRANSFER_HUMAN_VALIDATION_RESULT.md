2. The lower-right plated dish occupies too much visual mass; the piece still leans toward a product poster instead of a fully authored brand system.
3. The circular narrative windows and gold strokes read partially as collage/moodboard devices rather than one fully unified graphic grammar.
4. Small English labels/icons are generic and do not yet establish a proprietary typographic voice.
5. The dark field is overly brown/sooty in places and loses clarity; black-space hierarchy should be cleaner and more intentional.
6. Typography remains the largest absolute-quality bottleneck: readable and controlled, but not yet distinctive enough to function as brand-level display lettering.
