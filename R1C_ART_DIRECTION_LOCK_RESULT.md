## Locked Art Direction

Working name: `STOVE-RIM EDITORIAL`

The next design must use the frozen real-food asset and translate the actual pan / heat / chili structure into the graphic system.

### 1. Attention geometry
- 4:5 canvas.
- Food image is the visual anchor, but must not be placed as a plain rectangular photo under a headline.
- Use a strong asymmetrical crop so the pan occupies roughly 55–65% of the canvas and is partially cropped by the frame.
- The visual mass should bias to lower-right or lower-center, leaving deliberate breathing room in the upper-left / upper field.

### 2. Product-derived graphic mechanism
Use exactly one primary mechanism:

`PAN RIM ARC + HEAT TRAIL + CHILI POINT RHYTHM`

- derive one or two partial arcs from the real pan rim;
- use a restrained vertical/diagonal heat-trail line behavior to guide the eye from food toward title;
- use a very small number of chili-red dots/marks as secondary rhythm;
- all three elements must visibly relate to the actual dish and cooking process.

Do not use unrelated L-shapes, generic borders, arbitrary rectangles or decorative circles.

### 3. Typography behavior
- Use real editable Chinese type.
- Avoid one-line oversized grotesk treatment.
- Break `今日现烧` into an editorial composition, with one word/phrase clearly dominant and the others subordinate.
- Prefer a real Chinese display/serif/humanist face with some hand-made energy if available; if unavailable, use a real Chinese serif fallback rather than a generic heavy sans block.
- Supporting copy `锅气刚好，趁热吃` stays small and quiet.
- No fake English, no price, no QR, no decorative microcopy.

### 4. Color logic
Use only:
- warm mineral off-white / paper-like neutral;
- charcoal / near-black;
- deep fermented-chili red as the only accent;
- natural colors from the frozen food photograph.

Do not use bright orange.
Do not add extra greens/blues unless they are already in the photograph.
Accent red should remain a minority, not a large color block.

### 5. Image treatment
- Frozen raster only; no ImageGen, no inpaint, no repaint.
- Strong crop and scale are allowed.
- No fake smoke overlay.
- No artificial vignette meant to simulate premium mood.
- Do not suppress food realism in order to make the layout cleaner.

### 6. Whitespace and grid
- Use a real grid, but the viewer should not see a generic template.
- Maintain a clear breathing zone around the display title.
- Avoid the R1B pattern of `headline + colored L + rectangular photo`.
- Image edge, title cluster and pan-rim arc should interlock as one composition.

### 7. Supporting graphics
Maximum 3 small supporting marks total.
Any mark must be derived from:
- pan rim;
- heat path;
- chili/ingredient point rhythm.

No leaves, mountains, red seals, random icons, brush strokes or ornamental frames.

### 8. Texture
Do not add texture merely to appear premium.
If used, texture must be extremely restrained and only support a warm uncoated-paper feeling.

## Hard avoids

The next design is an automatic Art Direction failure if it becomes any of the following:
- food photo + two lines of text;
- food photo + generic colored geometric block;
- large orange L / rectangle;
- centered poster template;
- traditional-Chinese brush / ink / seal shortcut;
- brand styling unrelated to the pan, heat or ingredients;
- ImageGen-generated text or food;
- a second design variant created because the first looks weak.
