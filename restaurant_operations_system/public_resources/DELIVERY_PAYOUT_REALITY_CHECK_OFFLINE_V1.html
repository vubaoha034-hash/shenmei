How to read the numbers

Expected payout = gross order amount − merchant-funded discounts − commissions − platform fees − allocated ad cost − other merchant deductions. Payout gap = actual payout − expected payout. Variable contribution = actual payout − food cost − packaging cost. A $0 gap only means the amounts reconcile under your assumptions; it does not mean the order is profitable.

Keep all inputs in the same currency and the same settlement basis. Use merchant-side amounts only. Exclude taxes, tips, refunds or delivery charges you do not economically own from gross; or reconcile them as separately documented adjustments. Allocated advertising can be hard to attribute per order. Different settlement batches and reserve holds can cause timing gaps. This is not net profit and excludes labor, rent, utilities, financing, taxes and other expenses. Do not infer a platform's actual fee structure from the sample.
