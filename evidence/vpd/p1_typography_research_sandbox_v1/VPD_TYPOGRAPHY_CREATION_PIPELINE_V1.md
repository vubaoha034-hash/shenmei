## 1. Core separation: pipeline vs method

### Pipeline

The pipeline answers:

> In what order must a professional typography problem be solved?

The order may be stable before any single construction method is proven.

### Method

A method answers:

> How will this specific wordmark/glyph be constructed at this stage?

Examples include faithful lettering vectorization, component/stroke construction, typeface-outline customization, geometric construction, or variable-width centerline reconstruction.

No method becomes a VPD rule merely because it appears in professional practice or in this pipeline. Method promotion remains controlled by the sandbox admission firewall.

---

## 2. Seven high-leverage variables

To comply with the aesthetic charter, this pipeline does not expand into a large taste rulebook. The main visual variables are limited to seven:

1. **Glyph correctness and recognition** — the intended Chinese character must read immediately.
2. **Component hierarchy** — character, component, major stroke, secondary stroke, and counter relationships must remain intelligible.
3. **Stroke rhythm** — trajectory, thickness modulation, joins and terminals must feel intentional.
4. **Optical balance** — center of gravity, apparent size, overshoot, openings and visual weight must balance optically, not merely geometrically.
5. **Inter-glyph negative space** — the space between characters is treated as a designed shape.
6. **Semantic integration** — brand/product meaning may influence structural behavior but must not be pasted on as unrelated icon decoration.
7. **Surface/material treatment** — texture and color are separate layers admitted only after the black/white structure works.

These are design variables, not validator scores.

## 3. The creation pipeline

### Stage A — Define the typography job

Before drawing, classify the object:

- brand wordmark;
- campaign/display title;
- headline family;
- supporting text;
- body copy.

A wordmark is not required to behave like a text font. Supporting typography is not required to inherit every deformation of the wordmark.

**Exit condition:** one primary job is named; mixed objectives are separated.

### Stage B — Define one or two semantic authorities

Choose only the strongest relevant source(s):

- approved mother lettering pixels;
- product/process structure;
- material/craft behavior;
- historical or vernacular lettering;
- place/architecture/physical artifact.

Do not accumulate decorative metaphors.

**Exit condition:** semantic sources are named and their role is structural, not ornamental.

### Stage C — Select a candidate construction method

Choose the method because it fits the current problem, not because the tool exposes a feature.

Candidate-method evidence lives in the typography research sandbox. Different titles may use different construction methods while remaining under the same VPD design discipline.

Examples currently under research include:

- faithful mother-lettering vectorization;
- CJK component/stroke-system construction;
- mature typeface outline + surgical customization;
- geometric/modular construction;
- centerline + variable-width reconstruction;
- historical/material redraw.

**Exit condition:** exactly one bounded method test is opened with an explicit baseline, output budget, and fail condition.

### Stage D — Build the black/white skeleton first

No texture, gradient, shadow, glow, leaf overlay, decorative grain, or color rescue is allowed to compensate for a weak glyph skeleton.

For custom Chinese lettering, preserve useful hierarchy where possible:

`wordmark -> character -> component -> major stroke -> secondary stroke -> counter / negative space`

Avoid collapsing the entire wordmark into one production-hostile dense compound path when a cleaner structured representation is possible.

**Exit condition:** the glyph is correct and the underlying structure is inspectable.

### Stage E — Optical correction

After structural correctness, inspect and adjust:

- visual center of gravity;
- apparent size rather than nominal bounding-box size;
- curve overshoot where needed;
- stroke-weight relationships;
- opening/counter sizes;
- terminal balance;
- pair spacing and wordmark rhythm;
- negative-space shape between characters.

This is a design pass, not an automatic smoothing pass.

**Exit condition:** black/white actual pixels survive both close and distance viewing without relying on effects.

### Stage F — Black/white human gate

The skeleton must be reviewed on actual rendered pixels.

Two channels are separate:

1. **Correctness:** Is the intended Chinese character immediately readable?
2. **Taste:** Does it have enough design character and quality to justify continuing?

Correctness failure stops the method immediately. Taste failure archives the method/output as negative evidence unless there is a specifically authorized bounded correction.

**Exit condition:** human reviewer explicitly says the black/white structure is worth continuing.

### Stage G — Surface/material layer

Only after Stage F passes may surface treatment be tested.

Surface treatment should be independently controllable:

- real grain;
- print/ink/brush texture;
- material irregularity;
- restrained color separation;
- masks/overlays tied to the approved form.

Do not use gradients, shadows, outlines, blur, noise, or texture as a substitute for weak structure.

**Exit condition:** material improves character without lowering legibility or making the result look low/dirty/synthetic.

### Stage H — Context and scale test

Test the accepted title/wordmark in relevant conditions:

- isolated black/white;
- small display;
- poster scale;
- photographic background;
- near view and distance view.

Do not interpret one attractive close-up as proof of production robustness.

**Exit condition:** title remains correct, distinctive and usable in the actual design context.

### Stage I — Supporting typography hierarchy

Only after the title/wordmark passes may supporting typography be developed:

- subtitle;
- English/Latin companion;
- body/support copy;
- spacing hierarchy;
- scale contrast.

Supporting text should harmonize with the wordmark without copying all of its distortions.

**Exit condition:** support hierarchy is readable and does not flatten or compete with the approved title.

### Stage J — Poster reintegration

Only after title and support hierarchy pass may P6 reintegration resume.

Photography gains already earned are preserved. Reintegration must not regenerate accepted photographic bases merely to chase taste.

**Exit condition:** full poster is judged as a complete design on actual pixels; editable source evidence remains separate from aesthetic acceptance.

## 5. Method admission firewall

A candidate method may move from research to a VPD production rule only after all of the following:

1. bounded actual-project test;
2. preserved baseline;
3. no hidden variants or unrecorded retry advantage;
4. glyph correctness pass;
5. actual-pixel human visual acceptance;
6. explicit user acceptance that the visible improvement is worth keeping;
7. separate promotion commit naming exactly what enters which production authority.

A local pass may justify another validation. It does not prove universal applicability.

Failed methods remain useful negative evidence and must not be silently reintroduced under another name.
