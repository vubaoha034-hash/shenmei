## Absolute quality finding from independent review

The current lettering is clearly more than stock typesetting, but remains far below Mother Reference quality.

Main defects:

1. The four glyphs do not belong to one coherent lettering system.
2. `今` reads as an isolated symbol experiment rather than a mature opening glyph.
3. `日` is too mechanical and frame-like; arrow/peak terminals feel constructed rather than authored.
4. `现` is currently the strongest glyph, but still carries obvious font-derived structure.
5. `烧` is over-articulated and visually noisy; it needs subtraction, not more decoration.
6. Heat/fire semantics are currently applied as surface motifs rather than embodied in the glyph skeleton.
7. The group has customisation but lacks Mother Reference-level authority, recognition speed, silhouette control, and commercial impact.

## Refinement priorities

### P1 — Unify the system

The four glyphs must share a consistent authoring grammar:
- related terminal logic;
- related stroke-energy behavior;
- related heavy/thin contrast;
- coherent corner/cut treatment;
- consistent optical tension;
- coherent irregularity.

The result must not look like four separate experiments.

### P2 — Rebuild `今`

Problems to remove:
- icon-like top structure;
- weak lower support;
- disconnected horizontal/diagonal logic.

Required outcome:
- faster recognition;
- stronger base and centre of gravity;
- controlled asymmetry;
- one or two authored terminals, not many decorative tricks;
- silhouette capable of opening the title group with authority.

### P3 — Simplify `烧`

Problems to remove:
- too many competing hooks/drops/crossings;
- slow recognition;
- ornamental complexity not controlled by a strong skeleton.

Required outcome:
- subtract detail;
- preserve recognisability first;
- one dominant dynamic gesture maximum;
- simplify the fire radical and right-side structure into one coherent rhythm;
- the glyph must feel forceful, not busy.

### P4 — De-mechanise `日`

Problems to remove:
- rigid box/frame feeling;
- obvious engineered arrow/peak endpoints.

Required outcome:
- retain clarity;
- introduce human optical adjustment;
- reduce geometric literalness;
- integrate its terminal language with the other glyphs.

### P5 — Use `现` as a control glyph

`现` currently has the best weight and presence. Refine it without destroying this advantage.

Use it as the control reference for:
- perceived blackness;
- title mass;
- optical authority.

But remove obvious stock-font residue.

## Semantic rule

Do not add visible flame icons, droplets, smoke symbols, sparks, or other decorative motifs merely to imply heat.

`热 / 火 / 锅气` must be expressed through:
- stroke acceleration;
- tension;
- cut direction;
- terminal shape;
- compression / expansion;
- controlled asymmetry;
- overall silhouette.

Meaning should be embodied in the lettering, not attached to it.

## Recognition rule

Recognition speed is a hard gate.

A custom glyph that looks more dramatic but is materially harder to read is a regression.

`今日现烧` must remain legible at normal poster-title viewing size.
