## 1. Purpose

Prepare one compact renderer instruction for the already-defined V3-A R3A selective art-realism edit.

This patch does not change the R3A output budget, frozen composition, identity, typography plan, human acceptance authority, or downstream gates.

The patch only changes **how the next renderer call expresses the visual target**: fewer adjectives, stronger pixel-causal decisions, explicit density control, and physically plausible photographic behavior.

## 2. Six-control compiler

### 2.1 PRIMARY_VISUAL_CAUSE

The hero food must prove real cooked-material appetite first. Wok/process energy is supporting evidence of freshness and heat, not a spectacle layer.

### 2.2 ATTENTION_FLOW

`hero food surface -> credible wok/process evidence -> quieter dining/context exit`

The eye should not be pulled equally by particles, smoke, flame, garnish, people and food.

### 2.3 PROMPT_DENSITY_BUDGET

Hero food focal zone:
- use only 2–3 decisive material cues: irregular piece geometry, uneven sauce/moisture, varied sear/highlight behavior.

Process focal zone:
- use only 1–2 decisive cues: real wok heat/action and sparse steam/airborne evidence.

Supporting zones:
- may remain softer, darker, quieter and less explicit;
- no requirement for equal sharpness or equal visual interest.

### 2.4 CAPTURE_BEHAVIOR

`real high-end restaurant commercial photography`

Preserve the existing camera viewpoint and light direction. Target:
- natural highlight roll-off;
- moderate local microcontrast;
- non-uniform focus/texture response;
- believable oil/sauce reflections rather than glass-like gloss;
- no synthetic edge clarity across every object;
- no added film-effect fetish unless already present in the source image.

### 2.5 CONTROLLED_IMPERFECTION FAMILY

Use exactly one dominant realism family for this pass:

`food/material heterogeneity`

Express it through:
- slight piece-to-piece size and edge differences;
- unequal sauce thickness;
- mixed moist / matte / seared surfaces;
- different highlight intensity and oil-film reflection;
- irregular but plausible garnish distribution.

Do not add a second global “grime/noise/smoke/grain” imperfection family.

### 2.6 HARD AVOIDS — MAX 3

1. repeated plastic gloss or repeated food-piece geometry;
2. repeated garnish plus excessive suspended particles/sparks/droplets;
3. global over-sharpening, over-contrast, or orange/red cinematic spectacle.

3. garnish irregularity;

## 7. Failure policy

- a single failure is not sufficient to promote a permanent recipe;
- repeated human-confirmed failure may be recorded for later recipe promotion under existing evidence governance.
