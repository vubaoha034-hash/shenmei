# VPD V2 Photographer + Designer Self-Review Checklist

Use before any capsule is allowed to render.

## Photographer self-review

- Did I explain why the framing works, not only where the subject sits?
- Did I separate perspective/focal-length feel from exact unsupported camera metadata?
- Did I locate the focus plane and blur gradient?
- Did I describe key/fill/rim and negative fill, not merely call the light soft/hard?
- Did I describe shadow edge, density, falloff and specular behavior?
- Did I model highlight/midtone/black placement and local contrast?
- Did I model white balance plus regional warm/cool relationships?
- Did I describe saturation distribution and color-role hierarchy?
- Did I describe microcontrast, sharpening, grain/bloom/halation where visible?
- Did I describe subject styling, props, imperfection and environmental authenticity?
- Did I identify retouch/post operations that materially change the image?
- Did I mark uncertain optical/camera claims as inference?

Any NO on a material variable -> photography distillation is incomplete.

## Graphic designer self-review

- What is the image saying before I talk about style?
- What is read first, second and third?
- What is the grid/alignment logic?
- Is spacing optical or merely mathematically even?
- Is the typography stock, modified or custom lettering?
- Did I record actual size/weight/width/spacing relationships rather than saying `bold display type`?
- How does text interact with the image/crop/negative space?
- Does the wording itself drive the typographic treatment?
- What does the whitespace do?
- What are the color roles and approximate proportions?
- Are graphic elements structural or decorative?
- Where do motifs come from semantically?
- What crosses/bleeds/crops at the edge and why?
- What material/print behavior contributes to quality?
- If a set/campaign exists, what repeats and what deliberately varies?

Any NO on a material variable -> design distillation is incomplete.

## Art director integration review

- Do photo and type feel conceived together?
- Does the photo provide the right tonal/empty zone for type?
- Does crop anticipate typography?
- Does lighting support the intended hierarchy?
- Do graphic motifs belong to the subject/world?
- Is realism protected from over-stylization?
- Is the first-read strong at thumbnail scale?
- Is there a controlled second-read detail?
- Can I state the 4–10 highest-impact variables without reciting the whole schema?
- Can I state what is allowed to change without breaking the family?
- Can I state what must never be copied literally?

Any NO -> integration/transfer is not ready.

## Final anti-bloat review

If the compiled renderer prompt becomes a long essay, stop.

The schema is for analysis and memory. The renderer gets only the highest-impact controls, actual visual anchors, explicit freedoms and a short avoid list.
