# VPD V2 Changelog

## Why V2 exists

V1 was directionally correct but too abstract. It could describe a visual family at the level of `composition / color / lighting / typography`, yet still omit the professional controls that determine whether the final image actually looks right.

V2 adds the missing image-making depth.

## Added photography controls

- camera/view geometry;
- perspective/focal-length feel;
- focus plane and depth-of-field gradient;
- bokeh / motion blur / lens softness;
- key/fill/rim architecture;
- falloff, shadow hardness, specular rolloff;
- exposure placement and tone-curve behavior;
- white balance, hue/saturation/luminance role mapping;
- microcontrast, clarity, sharpening, grain, halation/bloom;
- subject styling, props, material response, imperfection;
- local retouch / dodge-burn / selective grade.

## Added design controls

- semantic intent;
- first/second/third read;
- grid, alignment axes and optical offsets;
- typography split into selection, scale system, lettering behavior and type-image interaction;
- copy meaning/tone;
- active/passive whitespace and density gradient;
- color-role proportions;
- structural vs decorative graphic language;
- z-order, bleed, crop and edge crossing;
- print/material behavior;
- campaign/sequence continuity.

## Added system controls

- observation vs inference confidence;
- causal priority map;
- variable interactions and tolerances;
- transferable style vs content/brand/production binding;
- multi-scale review;
- direct-baseline gate;
- second-style no-rewrite gate.

## Removed assumptions

V2 does not infer exact hidden camera settings from pixels without metadata.
V2 does not assume a more detailed prompt is better.
V2 does not treat technical correctness as aesthetic quality.
V2 does not move creative synthesis into Figma/Codex node construction.
