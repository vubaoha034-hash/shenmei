# R1D — INDEPENDENT PIXEL REVIEW

Status: `PASS_WITH_CAVEATS / PROVISIONAL_USABLE`
Date: `2026-08-14`

## Review scope

This review evaluates absolute visual usability of the single R1D proof. It does not infer quality from editability, Figma success, or Codex self-report.

## What materially improved vs R1B

1. The layout is no longer `food photo + headline + generic colored L`.
2. The pan is strongly cropped and occupies the lower visual field as a real visual mass.
3. The title hierarchy (`今日` subordinate / `现烧` dominant) creates an editorial composition rather than a standard header.
4. Warm off-white negative space is actively used for reading order and image tension.
5. The food, title, pan rim and supporting vectors now begin to behave as one composition.
6. The accent red is restrained and materially closer to the locked fermented-chili-red logic than the R1B orange block.

## Dimension review

### Food realism
`PASS`

The frozen real-food asset remains credible and materially stronger than the earlier ImageGen-first food outputs.

### Attention geometry / composition
`PASS_WITH_CAVEATS`

Strengths:
- strong lower-right/lower-field mass;
- partial pan crop works;
- real breathing space exists in the upper field;
- title and image are substantially more integrated than R1B.

Caveats:
- the top-right negative space is slightly under-resolved;
- the large pan crop is effective but close to becoming visually heavy at the lower/right edge.

### Typography hierarchy
`PASS_WITH_CAVEATS`

Strengths:
- clear dominant/subordinate relationship;
- title acts as visual mass rather than a normal header;
- the overall typographic direction is materially closer to Anchor 13.

Caveats:
- the exact spacing and optical balance of `今日` vs `现烧` still need production-level refinement;
- the supporting copy floats somewhat independently from the title/image system and is slightly too weak in contrast.

### Product-derived graphic mechanism
`PARTIAL_PASS / WEAKEST_AREA`

The pan-rim arc, heat trail and chili-point rhythm are present and are no longer arbitrary unrelated geometry.

However:
- the gray/red arcs still read partly as traced decorative lines rather than a fully proprietary brand mechanism;
- the heat trail is semantically understandable only after explanation; it does not yet feel inevitable from the cooking action;
- the chili points are restrained but not yet distinctive enough to become an ownable system.

### Brand distinctiveness
`PARTIAL_PASS`

The design has moved beyond generic poster template behavior, but the graphic language is not yet sufficiently proprietary to carry a full brand system by itself.

### Production readiness
`FAIL / NOT_FINAL`

This is not a final commercial master. It still needs a controlled refinement pass in typography, vector behavior and micro-spacing.

## Absolute quality decision

`PROVISIONAL_USABLE`

Definition used: the core visual concept could plausibly enter a real project without redesign from scratch; remaining work is refinement rather than replacement of the concept.

This is the first proof in the current recovery sequence that crosses that threshold in the independent review.

It is **not** `PRODUCTION_READY` and is **not** permission to expand to R2/packaging yet.

## Why this is not an automatic full PASS

The central remaining risk is that the product-derived vector mechanism is still more like a supporting graphic gesture than a durable, ownable brand language. If the user judges the image `UNUSABLE`, that verdict overrides this provisional reviewer classification and the system must remain before R2.
